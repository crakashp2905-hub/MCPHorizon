**Lesson Title:** Introduction to Fractions

**Objective:** Understand what fractions are, how they represent parts of a whole, and basic operations with simple fractions.

**Content:**

1. **What is a Fraction?**
   - A fraction represents a part of a whole.
   - It consists of two numbers: the numerator (top number) and the denominator (bottom number).
   - The denominator tells you how many equal parts the whole is divided into, and the numerator tells you how many of those parts are being considered.

2. **Types of Fractions**
   - Proper Fraction: Numerator < Denominator
     Example: 1/4 (one part out of four)
   - Improper Fraction: Numerator ≥ Denominator
     Example: 5/3 (five parts out of three, which can be simplified to a mixed number)
   - Mixed Number: A whole number and a proper fraction combined.
     Example: 2 1/4 (two wholes and one part out of four)

3. **Basic Operations with Fractions**
   - Addition and Subtraction:
     To add or subtract fractions, they must have the same denominator.
     Example: 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4
   - Multiplication:
     Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.
     Example: 1/4 × 2/3 = 2/12 (which simplifies to 1/6)
   - Division:
     To divide fractions, multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.
     Example: 1/4 ÷ 1/2 = 1/4 × 2/1 = 2/4 (which simplifies to 1/2)
